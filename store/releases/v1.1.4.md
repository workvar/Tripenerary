## v1.1.4

Play-ready signed Android release. Builds from the committed `android/` tree with the upload keystore (no `prebuild --clean`), and fixes tag → `versionCode` parsing for `v1.x.y` tags.

### Highlights

- **Upload-key signed AAB.** Release artifacts are verified not to be `CN=Android Debug` before publish.
- **versionCode fix.** `v1.1.4` correctly maps to `10104` (previous parser turned patch into `3.0.0` / empty code).
- **First Play upload candidate.** Use `tripenerary-1.1.4.aab` in Play Console; discard debug-signed `v1.1.2` bundles.
